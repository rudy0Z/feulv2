# Feul --- Flow Stories + Winning Design Moments

## Purpose

These are the moments the portfolio case study should explain.

The goal is not to show many screens.

The goal is to show why the product works.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 1. STORY ARC

## Act 1 --- "Can I trust this?"

The contributor does not begin with recording.

They first: - choose a language - understand what is being recorded -
understand who can access it - understand deletion rights - understand
that paid money is not clawed back after deletion

### Winning moment

**Consent in the contributor's language before microphone access.**

This turns consent from legal compliance into a product interaction.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 2. ACT 2 --- "Can I actually earn?"

Home does not sell a vague promise.

It immediately presents: - a real first job - language - format - time -
payout - what is required

### Winning moment

**The first earning path is visible and concrete.**

The user can understand how ₹100 is reachable.

The product does not require: - profile optimization - proposals -
interviews - streaks - hours tracking

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 3. ACT 3 --- "Why does this job pay this amount?"

A contributor sees:

**base × coverage multiplier + bonus = total**

The multiplier is not a mystery reward.

It communicates the marketplace economics:

> The platform needs this particular kind of coverage.

### Winning moment

**Coverage economics becomes visible before recording.**

This is one of the strongest product-thinking moments in the case study.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 4. ACT 4 --- "What exactly am I supposed to do?"

The Brief explains: - situation - expected behavior - language -
duration - sample - requirements - how the AI/director guides the
situation

### Winning moment

**AI disappears into the workflow.**

The contributor never needs to understand an AI architecture.

They understand the work.

This is deliberately different from building an AI chatbot.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 5. ACT 5 --- "Recording feels like work, not a toy."

Studio becomes visually distinct.

The interface gives: - clear script/prompt - real mic feedback -
progress - waveform - timing - pause/recovery - quality feedback

### Winning moment

**Waveform becomes functional product identity.**

Not wallpaper.

The waveform means: - sound is happening - audio can be reviewed -
recording is progressing - quality is being observed

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 6. ACT 6 --- "Something went wrong, but I didn't lose my work."

This is a critical trust moment.

Example:

**9/10 passed** **Fix 1 → ₹25**

The contributor sees exactly: - which clip failed - why - how to fix it

The accepted clips remain accepted.

### Winning moment

**Partial rejection becomes repair, not punishment.**

This is much stronger than a generic "Your submission failed" screen.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 7. ACT 7 --- "Where is my money?"

Wallet separates:

**Available** from **In review** from **This week** from **Total**

The ledger explains pending states.

### Winning moment

**Money states are explicit rather than ambiguous.**

"Waiting on validator review" is better than "Pending."

The product treats payment visibility as trust infrastructure.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 8. ACT 8 --- "How much do I need before I can withdraw?"

If the user has ₹82:

**₹18 more to withdraw**

The product calculates the gap.

### Winning moment

**Exact-gap withdrawal removes mental arithmetic.**

This is a tiny interaction with disproportionate trust value.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 9. ACT 9 --- "Did the money actually go?"

Withdrawal success includes:

-   amount
-   VPA
-   reference
-   UTR
-   timestamp
-   receipt

### Winning moment

**The receipt makes the payout verifiable.**

The interface does not stop at a green check.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 10. ACT 10 --- "What happens to my data?"

Profile/Data Vault lets the contributor revoke/delete recorded data.

If money was already paid:

**Data is deleted. Money is not clawed back.**

### Winning moment

**Data rights are connected to a real ledger consequence.**

This is a strong ethical/product-system story.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 11. ACT 11 --- "What if the campaign disappears?"

If a campaign closes after the contributor has submitted:

**The submission is still honoured and paid.**

The platform absorbs the campaign withdrawal.

### Winning moment

**The product protects contributors from marketplace uncertainty.**

This is a marketplace trust decision, not a visual feature.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 12. ACT 12 --- "What if I get interrupted?"

For a long ROOM recording:

**Your 19-minute take is saved. Resume within 24h.**

The Home screen surfaces the recovery state.

### Winning moment

**Failure recovery is part of the main journey, not hidden in
settings.**

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 13. VISUAL WINNING MOMENTS

The visual system should have a few memorable moments, not visual noise
everywhere.

## Moment A --- Language

Native scripts create identity.

Use large readable language names.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Moment B --- Earnings

Large tabular numerals.

Money should feel real, not decorative.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Moment C --- Coverage

The multiplier should feel like meaningful marketplace information.

Avoid casino-like reward styling.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Moment D --- Waveform

The waveform is Feul's signature.

Use different registers: - live - scrub - thumbnail - separator - studio
lanes

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Moment E --- Studio transition

Browsing is calm and neutral.

Entering Studio increases: - contrast - waveform presence - audio
feedback - focus

The product becomes more expressive exactly when audio work begins.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Moment F --- Earning credited

Quiet celebration.

Large amount.

One sentence.

No confetti.

The restraint communicates confidence.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 14. DELETED MECHANICS --- IMPORTANT CASE STUDY STORY

The product originally explored mechanics that were later removed.

Do not hide this.

### Removed

-   Security Reserve
-   streak mechanics

Why?

They introduced behavior that did not serve the actual marketplace/job
model.

The final system instead uses: - access - standing - settlement speed -
coverage demand - real work

This is a portfolio decision story.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 15. WHAT NOT TO TURN INTO A "WINNING MOMENT"

Do not visually over-celebrate:

-   AI
-   badges
-   coverage multipliers
-   profile standing
-   task counts
-   notifications
-   generic success toasts

The strongest moments are the ones where the interface resolves real
uncertainty.
