# Feul --- Figma Make Master Brief

## Purpose

Build the contributor-facing Feul mobile product as a
production-plausible 2026 consumer app.

This file is the source of truth for the Figma Make build. The attached
visual references are inspiration, not templates to copy.

The product is a **voice-data gig marketplace**, not an AI interview app
and not an AI chatbot.

## Product thesis

Feul connects Indian contributors with paid voice-data work.

-   AI/labs define the coverage need.
-   AI/director infrastructure generates or guides realistic situations.
-   Contributors perform the situations in their own language.
-   Quality systems validate submissions.
-   The marketplace distributes work.
-   Trust systems govern consent, review, payment, and data rights.
-   Contributors earn rupees and build standing.

## Core contributor journey

Understand opportunity → choose language → understand consent → find
first earning opportunity → perform guided situation → submit/repair →
get paid → build standing → unlock better work.

The first-session experience should make **₹100 reachable** through real
work.

## Product principles

1.  **Job, not quest**
    -   One-tap apply.
    -   No proposals.
    -   No job-application interviews.
    -   One session.
    -   24h resume.
2.  **Same work, same base**
    -   Total = round(base × coverage multiplier) + bonus.
    -   Standing changes access and settlement speed, not base pay.
3.  **AI is infrastructure**
    -   Do not make AI/chat/copilot the visual headline.
    -   The contributor is doing real work.
    -   AI directs the situation behind the scenes.
4.  **Trust moves money**
    -   Native-language consent before any microphone access.
    -   Clear recording/data rights.
    -   Review and repair states.
    -   In-flight jobs are honored if campaigns close.
    -   Wallet shows real money states.
5.  **Language is primary**
    -   Language/dialect should drive work discovery.
    -   Do not use location as a primary personalization mechanism
        unless a specific job genuinely requires geographic/acoustic
        context.
6.  **Buildable over spectacular**
    -   Every component must be implementable.
    -   Avoid decorative UI that exists only in an image.
    -   No fake 3D, excessive glass, random blobs, or impossible
        interactions.

## Product structure

Bottom navigation:

-   Home
-   Jobs
-   Wallet

Profile is accessed from the top-right avatar.

Studio is not a bottom-navigation destination. It is entered from Home
or Jobs.

## Portfolio scope

The Figma build should prioritize:

1.  Onboarding
2.  Home
3.  Jobs
4.  Job Detail
5.  Studio
6.  Wallet
7.  Profile

Do not expand into unrelated marketplace dashboards, admin tooling,
validator tooling, or AI chat surfaces.

## Success criterion

A designer reviewing the file should immediately understand:

> This is a serious contributor marketplace where people can discover,
> perform, and get paid for real voice-data work.

It should feel like a real product, not a concept-art dashboard.
